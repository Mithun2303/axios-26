ACHRONOS – Beyond Time

The word Achronos is inspired by the Greek word Chronos, which means time. We added the prefix "A", giving it the meaning "Beyond Time."
For us, Achronos is a world where time has no boundaries.
It is a place where the past, present, and future come together.
Why is this the best theme for AXIOS?
A symposium is about ideas and innovation. And every innovation has a journey. Today's knowledge stands on discoveries made years ago. Today's technology will inspire tomorrow's inventions. Innovation was never limited by time and that is exactly what Achronos represents.
What does "Beyond Time" mean?
It does not mean time travel. It means great ideas never go out of date. A discovery made hundreds of years ago still helps engineers today. The projects we build now may inspire someone years from now. Ideas live on, beyond the time they were created in. That is why our theme is Beyond Time


Why is this theme perfect for AXIOS?
Unlike many themes that are limited to one style or one story, Achronos gives us unlimited creative possibilities.

Ancient discoveries 
Modern technology 
Future innovations

Technical and non-technical events
